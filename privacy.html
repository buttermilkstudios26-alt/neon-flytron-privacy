Neon Flytron Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026 · App ID: com.buttermilkstudios.neonflytron

In short: Neon Flytron does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It needs no account, has no ads or analytics, requests no Android permissions and works fully offline.

Who we are

Neon Flytron ("the app") is a mobile game developed and published on Google Play by Buttermilk Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, the app:

  • does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account details;
  • does not request any Android permissions (no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access);
  • does not connect to the internet or send any data to us or anyone else;
  • contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs;
  • does not use the Android advertising ID or any other device identifier.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress and preferences, the app saves a small amount of game data only on your device:

DataPurpose
Best survival timeShows your personal best
Selected craftRemembers your chosen ship
Sound, haptics and reduced-effects settingsKeeps your preferences between sessions

This data never leaves your device through the app, and we have no access to it.

Android device backup

If you have turned on Android's backup feature, Android may include the game data above in your own device backup stored in your Google account. This is controlled by your device settings and Google's privacy policy. We cannot see or access these backups.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data the app has stored at any time by uninstalling the app, or by going to Settings → Apps → Neon Flytron → Storage → Clear data on your device.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third parties

We do not sell, trade or share any information with third parties. The app is distributed through Google Play. Google's handling of your Play Store account and downloads is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
